# HOA Rules You Didn't Know You Agreed To

When you buy into a homeowners association, you agree to a rulebook most buyers never read - the CC&Rs - and those rules reach further than the monthly fee. They can decide what colour you paint your door, where you park, whether you can rent the home out, what pets you can keep, and how long your bins can sit outside. The fee is only the visible part: the rules are the part that causes arguments later. Before you buy, read the CC&Rs, the bylaws and the meeting minutes from the last year - the minutes show which rules are actually enforced and which fights keep coming back. If a rule would change how you live, find out before you sign, not after the first fine letter. And check the fee against your budget first, because the rules and the fee come as one package.
