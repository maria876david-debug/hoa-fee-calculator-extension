# HOA Reserve Funds: The Savings Account That Protects You From Special Assessments

Every HOA keeps two kinds of money: the operating fund that pays this month's bills, and the reserve fund that saves for the big repairs - the roof, the road resurfacing, the pool rebuild. When the reserve fund is healthy, those repairs are already paid for. When it is empty, the board has one tool left: a special assessment, a sudden extra bill that can run into thousands per home. Before you buy into an HOA, ask for the reserve study and the current reserve balance. A community collecting a slightly higher monthly fee into a strong reserve is usually cheaper to live in than one with low fees and an empty reserve - the bill arrives either way, the only question is whether it arrives monthly or all at once.
