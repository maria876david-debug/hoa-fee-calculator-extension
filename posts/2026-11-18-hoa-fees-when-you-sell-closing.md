# HOA Fees When You Sell: Who Pays What at Closing

HOA fees do not stop at the sale - they follow the home, and the closing table is where buyers and sellers argue about them. Unpaid monthly fees and any outstanding special assessment are usually settled from the sale proceeds before the seller is paid, and the buyer should never accept a home without a written statement from the association showing a zero balance. The other cost is the transfer and move-in fee many associations charge the new owner, which can be a few hundred dollars and is easy to miss in the closing paperwork. Before you sign, ask for three documents: the current fee schedule, the resale certificate, and the association's financial statements. A seller who produces them quickly is a good sign; a seller who stalls is not.
