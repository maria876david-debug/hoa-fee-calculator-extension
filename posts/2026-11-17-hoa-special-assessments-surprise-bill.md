# HOA Special Assessments: The Surprise Bill Buyers Never Budget For

Monthly HOA fees are visible in every listing. Special assessments are not — and they are the bill that catches buyers off guard. A special assessment is a one-off charge the association levies for a big repair: a new roof on the shared building, repaving, a lift replacement. It can run into thousands of dollars and it lands whether you just moved in or have lived there for years. Before you buy into any HOA community, ask three questions: does the association have a healthy reserve fund, has it levied an assessment in the last five years, and is one planned in the next two? The answers are in the HOA's financial documents, and any seller who will not share them is telling you something.
