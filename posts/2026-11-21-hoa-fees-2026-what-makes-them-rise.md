# HOA Fees in 2026: What Actually Makes Them Go Up Each Year

HOA fees rarely stay still, and the increases are not random - they follow the same pressures as any household budget. Insurance is usually the biggest driver: premiums for the community's shared buildings have risen sharply, and one claim can push the next year's cost up for everyone. Then come the contracts - landscaping, pool care, pest control, security - which renew at higher rates each year. Add in deferred maintenance finally catching up, and a reserve fund that was never funded properly, and the board has little choice but to raise the monthly fee. What buyers should watch is not whether the fee goes up - most do - but why: a board that can show the insurance bill, the contract renewals and the reserve study is managing the money; a board that cannot explain the increase is a warning sign.
