# HOA Fees and Your Monthly Budget: The Number Buyers Forget to Check

Buyers check the mortgage, the taxes and the insurance - and then the HOA fee arrives every month on top of all of it. That fee pays for common areas, building maintenance and the reserve fund that covers big repairs like a new roof.

What catches buyers out is the part nobody quotes upfront: special assessments. When the reserve fund runs short, owners can be asked for a lump sum, sometimes thousands of dollars. Before you make an offer, run the numbers properly: monthly fee, what it covers, how healthy the reserve fund is, and whether assessments have been charged before.
