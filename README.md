# HOA Fee Calculator — Quick Estimate

Estimate annual HOA fees and cost per square foot in seconds.

A quick companion to hoafeecalculator.com. Estimate your annual HOA fees — and cost per square foot when you know the home size — without opening a new tab. All maths runs locally in the popup.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/hoa-fee-calculator-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://hoafeecalculator.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
